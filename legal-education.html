Section-by-Section Legal Curriculum

Prevailing Indian statutory acts covered in our daily educational YouTube series:

1. Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)
Sections 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 & 11-14 · Every Monday & Thursday 08:00 AM IST
28 Video Episodes

The definitive statutory framework governing data fiduciary accountability, unbundled consent architectures, notice obligations, and strict minor tracking prohibitions in India.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Section 4 & 5 Notice Requirements & Grounds for Processing Citizens must receive clear, itemized notices in 22 constitutional languages before data collection begins.
Section 6 Unbundled, Granular & Freely Revocable Consent Consent cannot be bundled. Users have an unconditional statutory right to revoke location tracking permissions anytime.
Section 8 General Obligations of Data Fiduciaries Mandatory data erasure upon withdrawal of consent, strict technical safeguards, and prompt breach notification.
Section 9 Absolute Protections for Children & Minors (< 18) Strict statutory ban on tracking, profiling, or targeted commercial advertising directed toward minors under 18.
Section 11-14 Rights of Data Principals & Redressal Right to access data summaries, correct inaccuracies, nominate representatives, and appeal to the DPBI.
2. Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023 (Modern Criminal Jurisprudence)
Sections 34-44, 63-79, 106 & 126-127 · Every Tuesday 08:00 AM IST
36 Video Episodes

Comprehensive breakdown of criminal statutes protecting individual bodily integrity, safety mandates for women and children, and emergency private defense rights.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Sections 34-44 Right of Private Defense of Body & Property Knowing the precise statutory boundaries where reasonable physical force is legally protected during imminent danger.
Sections 63-79 Offenses Against Women, Children & Bodily Dignity Stringent penal consequences for stalking (Sec 78), voyeurism (Sec 77), digital harassment, and unauthorized monitoring.
Section 106 Causing Death by Negligence & Road Incident Reporting Clear legal protection for citizens who promptly report road vehicular collisions to emergency responders.
Sections 126-127 Wrongful Restraint & Confinement Criminal penalties against illegal obstruction of movement and emergency habeas corpus reporting pathways.
3. Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023 (Citizen Procedural Safeguards)
Sections 35, 173 (Zero FIR) & 193 · Every Wednesday 08:00 AM IST
32 Video Episodes

Procedural rights empowering ordinary citizens during emergency police interactions, institutionalizing nationwide Zero-FIR registration, electronic complaint lodgment, and arrest safeguards.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Section 173 Mandatory Zero-FIR & Electronic FIR (e-FIR) Filing Police stations are statutorily mandated to register a Zero-FIR for any cognizable offense irrespective of where the crime occurred.
Section 35 & 36 Arrest Procedures & Prior Notice of Appearance Police cannot arbitrarily arrest for offenses with punishment under 7 years without issuing a prior Notice of Appearance under Section 35(3).
Section 193 Statutory Investigation Timelines & Citizen Updates Complainants have the statutory right to receive progress updates on their FIR investigation within 90 days.
4. Information Technology Act, 2000 (Amended)
Sections 43, 66, 66C, 66E & Section 79 · Every Saturday 08:00 AM IST
26 Video Episodes

Cybersecurity, digital fraud prosecution, hacking penalties, and the statutory obligations and safe-harbour exemptions of digital intermediaries operating in India.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Sections 43 & 66 Hacking, Data Theft & Malware Penalties Punitive measures and up to 3 years imprisonment for installing spyware, unauthorized phone tracking, or harvesting address books.
Sections 66C & 66D Identity Theft & Cheating by Digital Personation Stringent penal sanctions for impersonating recruiters or hijacking digital credentials.
Section 66E Privacy Violation & Digital Voyeurism Criminal liability for capturing or sharing private coordinates and photos without explicit consent.
Section 79 Intermediary Safe Harbour & Due Diligence Platforms must exercise due diligence, KYC verification, and prompt grievance resolution to qualify for intermediary safe harbour.
5. Motor Vehicles Amendment Act, 2019 & Good Samaritan Protections
Section 134A · Emergency Rescue Immunity · Every Sunday 08:00 AM IST
18 Video Episodes

Critical legal knowledge for vehicular travel, crash emergencies, ambulance right-of-way, and complete legal immunity for citizens aiding road accident victims.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Section 134A Good Samaritan Legal Immunity & Rights Citizens who aid accident victims are legally exempt from civil/criminal liability, police interrogation, or hospital detention.
Section 194E Failure to Yield to Emergency Vehicles Mandatory Rs 10,000 fine and imprisonment for blocking ambulances and fire response teams.
Section 161 Compensation Scheme for Hit-and-Run Victims Standardized compensation funds (Rs 2 Lakhs for death) for families during tragic unverified vehicular collisions.
6. Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Section 2(47), Section 35 & E-Daakhil Online Portal
22 Video Episodes

Mastering consumer rights in the digital age: tackling deceptive job advertisements, unauthorized recurring card subscriptions, and filing e-Daakhil consumer claims from home.

Section Subject / Provision Citizen Right / Practical Application
Section 2(47) Unfair Trade Practices & Dark Patterns Legal penalties for platforms making unsubstantiated safety claims or hiding automatic subscription renewals.
Section 35 & 38 E-Daakhil Online Consumer Complaints How ordinary citizens can file legal consumer grievances online without hiring high-priced lawyers.
