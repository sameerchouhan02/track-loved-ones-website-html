About Our Ecosystem

Engineered to balance unconditional family care with uncompromised human dignity.

Built for Family Protection, Not Data Mining

Track Loved Ones was founded on a simple truth: you shouldn't have to surrender your family's personal whereabouts to advertising cartels just to ensure your child arrived safely at school or your elderly parents reached home.

Track Loved Ones is the consumer brand of People Connect, the company that builds, operates and stands behind every part of this ecosystem.

We eliminated persistent tracking databases. In our ecosystem, coordinates flow directly in encrypted volatile memory and dissolve the moment the mutual session concludes. No location history is ever archived or monetized.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding privacy, 24-hour expiry, battery consumption, and our educational business model.

Why does tracking permission automatically expire after 24 hours? ▾
We believe continuous, perpetual tracking should never become an unmonitored default. Requiring a fresh mutual connection request every 24 hours guarantees that both individuals remain fully conscious of and in agreement with location sharing, honoring constitutional privacy norms.
How is the Track Loved Ones app funded if it is completely free? ▾
We do not sell personal data, display intrusive ads, or charge monthly subscription fees to families. Instead, our operational and server infrastructure is fully funded through the monetization of our daily educational YouTube video series covering Indian legal acts and citizen rights section-by-section.
Do you store my location coordinates on your servers? ▾
No. Our architecture uses Zero-Location-Storage. Coordinates are processed transiently in volatile system memory solely for real-time relay to the family member you chose to connect with. Once delivered to your family member's screen, the telemetry is discarded and never written to hard drives.
How do I connect with a loved one? ▾
You choose who you wish to connect with, and we send the invite on your behalf. Once they accept, you are connected, without any tedious multi-step pairing.
