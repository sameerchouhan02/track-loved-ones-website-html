Corporate KYC Verification & Anti-Fraud Policy

Strictly Non-Refundable · INR 250 Fee

Under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, Track Loved Ones acts strictly as an intermediary. To uphold our safe harbour status and shield prospective commercial partners and job-seekers from fraudulent shell companies, every entity undergoes corporate identity checks before posting:

1. Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) Validation Verification of corporate CIN, GSTIN registration, active bank mandate, and registered office physical address in India.
2. Authorized Officer Verification PAN card and authorized signatory verification of the business director, partner, or recruiter posting opportunities on the platform.
Mandatory Fee Notice: Because this nominal charge directly covers non-recoverable third-party identity API calls and verification costs, the INR 250 fee is non-refundable once initiated, regardless of verification outcome.

Prohibited Conduct Policy for Businesses & Employers

Users, corporate recruiters, and businesses are strictly prohibited from soliciting private location tracking links from candidates or users through marketplace posts, publishing deceptive job descriptions or fake dealership claims, demanding unauthorized application fees, or attempting to monitor minors.

Any violation results in immediate forfeiture of corporate KYC status, lifetime banning from People Connect, and mandatory reporting to Indian law enforcement authorities.
