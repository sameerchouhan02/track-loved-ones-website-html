Statutory Recruitment Standard

Corporate KYC Verification & Anti-Fraud Policy

Strictly Non-Refundable · Flat INR 250 Fee

Under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, Track Loved Ones acts strictly as an intermediary. To uphold our safe harbour status and shield job-seekers from fraudulent postings, every business entity undergoes third-party biometric and corporate identity checks before posting:

1. Legal Entity Validation (MCA Registry) Verification of corporate CIN (Ministry of Corporate Affairs), GSTIN registration, active bank mandate, and registered office physical address in India.
2. Authorized Officer Verification PAN card and authorized signatory verification of the corporate recruiter, business owner, or HR director posting positions on the platform.
Mandatory Fee Notice: Because this nominal charge directly covers non-recoverable third-party identity API calls and verification costs, the INR 250 fee is non-refundable once initiated, regardless of verification outcome.

Prohibited Conduct Policy for Recruiters & Employers

Users and corporate recruiters are strictly prohibited from soliciting private location tracking links from candidates or users through marketplace posts, publishing deceptive job descriptions, demanding unauthorized application fees from candidates, or attempting to monitor the behavior of minors.

Any violation results in immediate forfeiture of corporate KYC status, lifetime banning from the People Connect network, and mandatory reporting to Indian law enforcement authorities.
