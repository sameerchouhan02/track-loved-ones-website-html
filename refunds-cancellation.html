Refunds & Cancellation Policy

This document establishes the transaction terms, subscription renewals, and cancellation frameworks for users of the Track Loved Ones app and business directory modules.

1. Corporate KYC & Listing Verification Fees

To keep the marketplace safe from employment fraud, all recruiters, employers, and entrepreneurs undergo mandatory background identity checks before listing positions.

Payment Item Statutory Charge Policy Status
KYC Processing & Corporate Verification INR 250 (Inclusive of all statutory taxes) Strictly Non-Refundable

Because the fee pays for third-party automated identity checking services, the INR 250 fee cannot be refunded once processing starts, even if the business fails the verification check.

2. App Subscription Tiers & Cancellation

Family location safety packages follow monthly or annual terms with a 7-day trial period. You can stop future automatic renewals anytime through your Apple App Store or Google Play Store account settings. Stopped plans remain fully functional until the current billing cycle ends.
