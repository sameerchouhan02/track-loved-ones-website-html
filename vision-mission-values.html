Vision, Mission & Core Values

The philosophical and statutory principles driving our family safety ecosystem, sovereign data architecture, and citizen empowerment initiatives.

Our Long-Term Aspiration

Our Vision

A world where every family stays seamlessly connected and safe without sacrificing personal dignity, surveillance intrusion, or data privacy. We envision technology as an enabler of trust, empowering citizens with transparent control over their digital footprints and eliminating fear through constitutional safety protections.

✓ Dignity-first, consent-driven safety for all families
Our Operational Commitment

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, sub-meter peer-to-peer location safety and emergency assistance that strictly complies with constitutional citizen rights and Indian legal mandates. We democratize family protection by providing our core app 100% free—funded ethically through public legal literacy on YouTube rather than data commodification or surveillance capitalism.

✓ Funded by public legal education, not data selling

Our Non-Negotiable Core Values

Six foundational pillars governing every line of code, server deployment, and legal policy we establish.

🤝

1. Uncompromising Human Dignity & Consent

Every citizen holds unalienable ownership over their physical location. We reject perpetual covert tracking: our mandatory 24-hour permission auto-expiry standard guarantees continuous, conscious mutual consent.

🔒

2. Zero Data Commercialization

We never sell, lease, or monetize personal coordinates to data brokers, ad networks, or third parties. Our Zero-Location-Storage architecture processes live telemetry in dynamic memory, leaving zero disk movement trails.

⚖️

3. Legal Literacy as a Public Good

We believe knowledge of the law is a citizen lifeline. By funding our infrastructure through educational YouTube videos on prevailing Indian statutes, we keep safety free while building an informed citizenry.

🇮🇳

4. Domestic Data Sovereignty

In strict compliance with the DPDP Act 2023, we refuse public foreign clouds. All verification parameters and secure profile tokens reside exclusively on dedicated physical in-house servers located inside India.

🚨

5. Critical Lifeline Reliability

When distress strikes, technology must not fail. Single-tap SOS broadcast overrides silent ringers, launches turn-by-turn navigation for responders, and relays coordinates to national helplines in under two seconds.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

6. Multi-Generational Family Harmony

Engineered with empathy for teenagers seeking independence (via Blur & Ghost modes) and elderly grandparents requiring medical check-ins, balancing protective safety with personal privacy.

Our Statutory Guarantee to Citizens

We hold ourselves accountable under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, the Information Technology Act, 2000, and prevailing Indian telecom guidelines. If our practices ever diverge from our values, any user has the statutory right to request immediate data erasure and lodge a formal inquiry with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.
