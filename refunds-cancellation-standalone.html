Refunds & Cancellation Policy

This policy explains the current payment, verification, app-access and job-listing terms for Track Loved Ones. These terms may be updated if our services or pricing change.

1. End-User App Subscription

As of the date of this policy, Track Loved Ones does not charge end users any app subscription fee. There are no monthly or annual subscription plans, free trials, or automatic subscription renewals for end users at present.

2. Daily Law-Awareness Video for Continued App Use

To continue using the app, an end user is required to watch one law-awareness video each day. The daily video may be watched either within the app or through our official YouTube channel. This daily video requirement is an app-use condition and is not a subscription or subscription payment.

Exemptions: The daily law-awareness video requirement does not apply to child accounts or corporate accounts.

3. Corporate Verification & Job Listings

Companies must complete the applicable verification process before they can publish job listings. Job listings are currently free for verified companies. No job-posting fee is being charged at present.

4. Corporate Verification Fee, Refunds & Cancellation

The corporate KYC processing and verification fee remains INR 250 (inclusive of all applicable statutory taxes). This fee is separate from job-listing fees; verified companies can currently post job listings free of charge.

Payment Item Fee Refund Status
Corporate KYC Processing & Verification INR 250 (inclusive of all applicable statutory taxes) Non-refundable once processing starts

The INR 250 fee covers third-party identity-verification services. Once the verification process has started, the fee is non-refundable, even if the company does not pass the verification check, subject to applicable law.

Since there is currently no end-user app subscription, there is no subscription cancellation or recurring subscription payment to stop. If any paid service is introduced in the future, its price, payment, cancellation and refund terms will be displayed before the user or company makes a payment.

5. Right to Modify, Amend or Discontinue

People Connect reserves the right, at its discretion, to modify, amend, update, add to, suspend, restrict or discontinue any part of the Track Loved Ones app, its features, services, content, eligibility criteria, policies, terms and conditions, fees or operational procedures, at any time, subject to applicable laws and regulations. Where required by applicable law, or where a change materially affects users, People Connect will provide notice through the app, its website, email or another appropriate communication channel. Continued use of the app or services after an updated policy or terms take effect will be subject to the revised terms, to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in this clause limits any rights that users may have under applicable law.
